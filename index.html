Consumer Psychology & Decision Science

So... why did you actually buy that?

Hey Consumer Lab studies the everyday psychology of shopping, choice, and consumption — from a two-second grocery pick to trusting an AI's advice. We combine behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and real-world field studies to find out.

Why do we trust a stranger's five-star review more than our own judgment?

Can an algorithm nudge us toward better choices — or just more of them?
