FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior research experience?

No. We look for curiosity, reliability, and a willingness to learn — training is provided on the job.

Is this a paid or credit position?

Both options are typically available for RAs, depending on department policy and lab funding — ask when you apply.

What's the expected time commitment?

Most RA roles ask for 8–10 hours per week during the semester, with flexibility around exams and breaks.

How do I apply?

Email us with a short note about your interest, your resume/CV, and your availability. We'll follow up to schedule a conversation.
